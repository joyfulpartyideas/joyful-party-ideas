Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Joyful Party Ideas (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you visit this website, and how we use it.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account to read this site. We collect only limited information:

  • Information you give us: if you email us, we receive your name, email address and the contents of your message. We use this only to reply to you.
  • Basic usage information: like most websites, our hosting provider may log general, non-identifying information such as the pages you visit, your browser type and approximate location, to keep the site working and secure.

Cookies and advertising

This site may use cookies, including cookies from third-party services such as Google AdSense, if advertising is shown in future. These cookies may be used to show relevant ads and to measure how the site is used. You can control cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of personalised advertising through Google's Ads Settings.

Links to other websites

Some posts may link to other websites, such as shops or Pinterest. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites — please read their own privacy policies.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete an email you sent us, or ask what information we hold about you, by emailing futuristiccat3@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top will show when it was last updated.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us.
